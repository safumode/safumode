01 / THE BOUNDARY

One vault.
Two different authorities.

Your everyday wallet can fund and relay transactions, but cannot authorize withdrawals. A separate authorizer signs withdrawal requests. The recovery authority can recover only to the fixed recipient, cancel pending exits, pause, co-approve trust and rotations, and veto a pending rotation. The current contract is an unaudited prototype.

02 / TRUSTED RECOVERY

Delay the exits that matter.

Withdrawals to untrusted addresses wait for the configured delay. Either authority can cancel a pending exit. Adding a trusted recipient requires both signatures and delayed activation. Either authority can pause; resuming requires both. Rotation requires both signatures, waits through the delay, and either current authority can veto it. A recovery transfer to the fixed recovery recipient is immediate. Monitoring is not implemented.
