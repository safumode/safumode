01 / THE BOUNDARY

One vault.
Two different authorities.

Your everyday wallet can fund and relay transactions, but cannot authorize withdrawals. A separate authorizer signs withdrawal requests; an independent recovery signer can cancel pending exits. The current contract is an unaudited prototype.

02 / TRUSTED RECOVERY

Delay the exits that matter.

Withdrawals to untrusted addresses wait for the configured delay. The recovery signer can cancel a pending exit. Adding a trusted recipient requires both signatures and delayed activation; a recovery transfer to the fixed recovery recipient is immediate. Monitoring is not implemented in this prototype.
